Tourism data sources

Mapped coordinates and identities: OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Route estimates use OSRM over OpenStreetMap roads. They are not live traffic or fares.

Download the derived expansion database and field sources. OSM-derived data is available under ODbL 1.0. Property-published tariff facts are individually attributed. No property photographs are redistributed without a separate license.

Prices are indicative or historical, not booking quotes. In particular, Hotel Good Night's cited tariff is effective February2014. Unknown prices, ratings, hours and visit durations remain unknown.
