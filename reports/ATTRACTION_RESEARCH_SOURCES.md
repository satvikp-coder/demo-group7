## Verification limits

All56 new fees and ratings remain null. All new raw duration fields remain null; separate planning metadata labels estimated durations. Published split operating windows and weekly closures are applied only where archived. Unknown hours are not24-hour access. Wildlife/wetland/monsoon/permit advisories remain provisional, not confirmed entry.

Saputara Echo Point, Nageshwar Mahadev and Rose Garden rely on Wikivoyage community identity/location; the university tourist list does not independently verify those three. Other source distinctions are preserved per record in curated-overrides.json.

Two official Dwarka beach map references were inconsistent with the coast. Cached OSM point references replace those coordinates, retaining explicit approximate-location qualifiers. A same-name Gopi Talav result in Surat was rejected; Dwarka identity/context and mapped point were checked separately. Ambiguous/missing-coordinate candidates were omitted (research-limitations.json).

OpenStreetMap-derived facts: ODbL1.0, attribution and derived release download in public/data-credits.html. Wikivoyage-derived points: CC BY-SA4.0. Factual source pages do not confer photograph reuse rights; all56 new images intentionally remain fallback.

Eight cached FOSSGIS OSRM driving-table matrices plus one corrected Dwarka table are actual provider results, not fabricated routes. They supply modeled road distance/time estimates, not live traffic, access guarantees or road geometry. Geometry remains the existing on-demand map service. One-off cached geographic lookups were serialized; no runtime Nominatim dependency was added.

A150-metre coordinate-proximity review found two pairs: Rose Garden/Saputara Museum and Gomti Ghat/Dwarkadhish Temple. They are distinct named sub-sites, not duplicated attractions; see proximity-review.json. Adjacent monuments are not collapsed merely because approximate map points are close. Normalized names, slugs and per-place source identities were also checked.
